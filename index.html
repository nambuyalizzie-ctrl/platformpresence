Versed in ValueThe thinking

Don't just count costs. Understand value.

Versed in Value is a way of reading worth across your money, your work and your character, because I think the same few principles run through all three.

Three kinds of value, one set of principles

Most advice treats money, career and ethics as separate subjects with separate rules. In my experience they behave more like one system: the habit that grows your savings is the same one that builds expertise and trust, and a shortcut that looks cheap in one area tends to cost you in another.

Every principle is read three ways:

Economic value

What is this worth? The difference between price, what you pay, and value, what you get.

Professional value

What problem do I solve? Being valued for the problems you solve rather than the hours you put in.

Ethical value

What do I stand for? The filter that stops a short-term gain from costing you long-term trust.

The toolkitThe practice

Put it to work

Value nobody can see tends to go unrewarded. These tools are free, small enough to use today, and built to help you show what you're worth, or what something is worth to you.

The value audit

A decision worksheet

Three questions to put to any decision, purchase or job offer before you commit, one for each kind of value. Answer them on the page and leave with a result you can print.

Use the value audit

Say it louder

A method and a line builder

Where Platform Presence came from

I started Platform Presence in 2020 as a small digital branding studio, building websites and social media for organisations.

The work reinforced something I already knew: presence without value is noise, and value nobody can see goes unrewarded.

Platform Presence now works on both halves of that. Versed in Value is about understanding and building real value; the toolkit is about getting it seen.
