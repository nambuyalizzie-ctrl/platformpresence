Where Platform Presence came from

I started Platform Presence in 2020 as a small digital branding studio, building websites and social media for organisations in Uganda and the UK.

The work taught me something I'm still thinking about: presence without value is noise, and value nobody can see goes unrewarded. Platform Presence now works on both sides of that, one project at a time.
