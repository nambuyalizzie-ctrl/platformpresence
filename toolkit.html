Tools for making your value visible.

Value nobody can see tends to go unrewarded. These are free, small enough to use today, and built to help you show what you're worth, or what something is worth to you.

The value audit

Use it here

Three questions to put to any decision, purchase or job offer before you commit. They come from Versed in Value.

  1. What is this worth over its whole life, not just today?
  2. What problem does it solve, and for whom?
  3. What does choosing it say about what I stand for?
